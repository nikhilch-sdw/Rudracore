The Challenge

Outdated Real Estate Portals & Slow MLS Sync

Horizon Properties needed a modern web & mobile app to stand out in high-end luxury real estate market. Existing IDX feeds were laggy, 3D property tours required heavy plugins, and mobile lead conversion was below 1%.

  • MLS Feed Latency: 6-hour delay in property status updates.
  • Heavy 3D Plugins: Virtual tours crashing on mobile Safari and Chrome.
  • Lead Leakage: Manual agent assignment taking over 2 hours per buyer inquiry.
The Solution

Flutter, WebGL & Automated MLS Pipelines

Rudracore built a cross-platform Flutter mobile app paired with a Three.js / WebGL 3D property rendering engine and automated sub-minute MLS IDX sync serverless pipelines.

  • WebGL 3D Virtual Tours: Browser-native 3D room navigation with 60fps mobile speed.
  • Sub-Minute MLS Sync: Instant property price changes and active/sold status alerts.
  • AI Lead Dispatch: Automated buyer-agent matching in under 30 seconds.
