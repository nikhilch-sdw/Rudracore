Common Inquiries

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our custom engineering services, SaaS platforms, squad engagement models, and delivery standards.

What software engineering and development services does Rudracore provide?

Rudracore Technologies delivers end-to-end technology solutions across custom web application development, iOS and Android mobile apps, enterprise cloud architecture (AWS, GCP, Azure), AI workflow automation, UI/UX product design, and dedicated engineering squad augmentation.

What proprietary products does Rudracore Technologies develop?

Beyond bespoke engineering services, Rudracore builds and maintains proprietary platforms including Chatwings (automated WhatsApp Business messaging platform), SEOWings (AI-powered SEO analytics and audit suite), SBN News Network (high-performance digital news publishing platform), and QR Wings (enterprise dynamic QR generation & campaign tracking).

How does intellectual property (IP) and source code ownership work?

You retain 100% full intellectual property ownership of all custom software, source code, database architectures, APIs, and design deliverables from day one. We never implement vendor lock-in, recurring licensing fees, or proprietary dependencies on your custom codebase.

What engagement models and squad structures do you offer?

We provide two flexible engagement structures: Dedicated Engineering Squads (senior developers, designers, and QA engineers working seamlessly as an extension of your internal team) and Fixed-Scope Milestone Projects (turnkey product delivery managed end-to-end from discovery to production launch).

How do your engineering squads communicate and report project milestones?

We operate in structured 2-week agile sprint cycles with bi-weekly sprint reviews, sprint planning, and daily standups. Our engineers integrate directly into your workspace via Slack, Microsoft Teams, Jira, GitHub, or Linear, ensuring complete progress transparency, predictable timelines, and weekly live demos.

How do you ensure enterprise data security, code quality, and compliance?

We adhere to strict ISO 27001-aligned security protocols, automated vulnerability scanning, SOC 2 compliant cloud patterns, and zero-trust data encryption both in transit and at rest. Every release undergoes peer reviews and automated CI/CD test pipelines with 90%+ code coverage before production deployment.
