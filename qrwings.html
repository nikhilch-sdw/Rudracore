Why Smart Review Routing & Local Reputation Matter

In competitive local markets, a single-star increase on Google Business Profile can increase organic venue revenue by up to 9% to 14%. However, relying on paper receipts or email follow-ups yields less than 2% response rates because the feedback moment has already passed.

QR Wings intercepts customer sentiment right at the counter, table, or clinic checkout using instant contactless NFC stands and dynamic QR codes.
