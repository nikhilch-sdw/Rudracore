Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about Google review velocity, NFC hardware, and compliance.

Does QR Wings comply with Google's Review Gating guidelines?

Yes. QR Wings follows Google Business Profile compliance rules. We provide an intuitive option for all customers to leave a public review, while creating a seamless direct channel for unhappy customers to communicate their concerns directly to management first.

Do customers need to download an application to scan and leave a review?

No. Scanning a QR Wings code or tapping an NFC stand opens directly inside the phone's native camera and browser (Safari, Chrome) in milliseconds. It requires zero app installation or signup.

How does an increase in review velocity improve our Google Maps ranking?

Google's local algorithm heavily prioritizes Review Recency, Velocity, and Sentiment. Steady weekly 5-star reviews with rich keyword mentions (like 'best pasta in town' or 'painless dental root canal') signal active topical authority, pushing your listing into the top 3 Google Maps positions.

What hardware formats are provided for venue deployment?

We supply UV-printed, scratch-resistant acrylic table stands, contactless NFC smart discs, and brushed metal wall plaques engineered to withstand heavy restaurant and clinic traffic.
