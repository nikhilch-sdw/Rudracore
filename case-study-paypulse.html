The Challenge

Handling Peak Payment Surges & Fraud Security

PayPulse needed to re-engineer their legacy payment switch to handle Black Friday transaction spikes, prevent double-charge race conditions, and achieve strict PCI-DSS audit compliance.

  • Database Lockouts: Race conditions during concurrent payment authorizations.
  • Fraud Risks: High fraudulent chargeback rate on international credit cards.
  • Reconciliation Delay: End-of-day bank settlement reconciliation taking 8+ hours.
The Solution

Go Microservices & Double-Entry Ledger Engine

Rudracore designed a high-throughput Golang payment switch with immutable double-entry ledger architecture, automated ML fraud scoring, and tokenized PCI Vault storage.

  • Immutable Ledger Architecture: Zero-discrepancy financial accounting with idempotent transactions.
  • AI Fraud Scoring: Real-time risk assessment reducing chargebacks by 85%.
  • PCI Tokenization Vault: AES-256 cardholder data isolation exceeding PCI-DSS Standards.
