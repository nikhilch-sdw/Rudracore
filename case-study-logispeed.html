The Challenge

Inefficient Routes & Unmonitored Idle Times

LogiSpeed Global suffered from unoptimized driver routes, excessive engine idling, and blind spots in cargo temperature tracking during cross-country freight transport.

  • Fuel Waste: Unnecessary idling costing $180,000+ annually.
  • Cold Chain Spoilage: Lack of real-time temperature telemetry alerts.
  • Manual Dispatch: Dispatchers relying on phone calls for driver ETA updates.
The Solution

IoT Sensors, MQTT Streaming & AI Routing

Rudracore deployed an MQTT telemetry ingestion pipeline on AWS, connected to cross-platform mobile apps for drivers and a web-based dispatch control tower.

  • Automated Route Optimization: Dynamic traffic-aware routing saving up to 45 mins per trip.
  • IoT Cold Chain Sensors: Instant SMS/Push alerts if cargo temperature strays from SLA.
  • Offline Mobile App: Driver app recording proof of delivery signatures offline.
