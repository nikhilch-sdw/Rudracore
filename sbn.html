Frequently Asked Questions

Everything news publishers and media leaders need to know about integrating SBN.

How does SBN achieve sub-50ms breaking news push dispatch?

SBN replaces legacy database polling with distributed in-memory Go ingest engines connected directly to an Apache Kafka messaging fabric. Once an editorial desk publishes an article, our WebSocket fan-out architecture dispatches push triggers to subscriber edges in milliseconds without waiting for batch runs.

Can SBN convert written articles into natural audio news podcasts automatically?

Yes. SBN incorporates a neural text-to-speech engine configured with broadcast journalism inflections. It automatically parses article body text, strips out markup or photo captions, and synthesizes crisp audio streams playable on lock-screens and headphones.

Does SBN support monetization and native ad networks?

Absolutely. SBN includes native in-stream ad slots that integrate seamlessly with Google Ad Manager, Prebid Mobile, and direct sponsor placements without impacting app scroll smoothness or battery life.

Can publishers license SBN as a white-label mobile app?

Yes. Digital publishers can license the SBN engine under their own branded app identity on both Google Play Store and Apple App Store, complete with custom typography, brand palette, and editorial CMS integration.
